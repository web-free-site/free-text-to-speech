Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Text and audio

Your typed text is processed in the browser for speech synthesis. We do not operate a backend that receives your content for TTS.

Local storage

Downloaded Piper model files may be stored in IndexedDB on your device to speed up repeat visits. You can clear site data in your browser to remove them.

Third parties

We use Adsterra for advertising; those partners may set cookies or collect usage data under their policies. Voice models are fetched from Hugging Face when you first use a voice.

Contact

Questions: see our contact page.
