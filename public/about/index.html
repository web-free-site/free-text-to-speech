Free Text to Speech

Neural text-to-speech in your browser — private, free, no account.

About

Free Text to Speech (free-text-to-speech.web-free.site) lets you type text, hear it, and download WAV audio with no account and no server-side synthesis.

Upstream project

This site is derived from clowerweb/piper-tts-web-demo, licensed under the Apache License 2.0. The original LICENSE file is included in our repository.

Voices we offer

  • en_US-ljspeech-medium — public domain (LJ Speech). Hugging Face
  • en_US-joe-medium — CC0. Hugging Face
  • fr_FR-mls-medium — CC BY 4.0 (MLS). Attribution in MODEL_CARD when redistributing audio derived from training data.

We do not ship lessac-, libritts-, or Swahili (lanfrica) voices because they are not on Piper's commercial-use list.

Piper & datasets

Neural inference uses Piper and models from rhasspy/piper-voices. See commercial-use documentation for licensing context.
